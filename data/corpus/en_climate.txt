Climate Change and the Energy Transition

Climate change is driven largely by greenhouse gas emissions from fossil fuels. Carbon emissions from electricity generation remain the largest source of greenhouse gas worldwide.

The energy transition toward renewable energy is essential to curb carbon emissions. Solar power and wind power have become the cheapest sources of new electricity, and the energy transition also needs grid storage.

Policy also matters. A carbon price makes fossil fuels reflect their true cost, while subsidies accelerate renewable energy deployment. Without rapid emissions cuts, climate change will intensify.
