The city council approved the new budget on Monday. The vote was close. Seven members voted in favor, and five voted against. The budget includes funding for three new schools. It also sets aside money for road repairs in 2026. Mayor Anna Lee called the decision a victory for local families. The budget will raise spending by 8 percent.

Opposition leaders disagreed strongly. They said the plan would raise taxes for small businesses.

The council debated the budget for six hours. Residents can attend the meeting and speak for two minutes each. The meeting starts at 7 p.m. in Room 204. More than 300 people attended the last meeting. A final decision on the park project is expected in December. The park project will cost 2 million dollars. Officials promised to publish the full report online. The report will be available next Friday.
